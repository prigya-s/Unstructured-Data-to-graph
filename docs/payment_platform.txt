# Payment Platform Documentation

## Overview

The Payment Platform is composed of the Checkout Service, the Payment Gateway
API, and the Ledger Database.

## Checkout Flow

The Checkout Service uses the Payment Gateway API to authorize payments.
The Checkout Service depends on the Inventory Service to confirm stock
availability before checkout completes.

## Data Storage

The Ledger Database stores all financial transactions. The Ledger Database
connects to the Analytics Database for nightly reporting.

## Ownership

The Payments Team owns the Checkout Service and the Payment Gateway API.
The Platform Engineering Team owns the Ledger Database.

## Compliance

The Payment Platform implements the PCI Compliance Policy. The Checkout
Service references the PCI Compliance Policy for audit purposes.
